The idea

Research is connected.

Papers point to projects. Projects produce datasets. Researchers gather around questions. Conferences create new connections.

The approach

Make the links visible.

ResearchGrid is a static concept for presenting scholarly discovery with strong hierarchy, useful metadata and an editorial point of view.

Frequently
asked questions.

Information for researchers, universities, and open science contributors.

How are research papers indexed on ResearchGrid?

Papers are aggregated from verified open-access archives, DOI registries, and institutional repositories using automated metadata extraction.

Can research labs submit their own datasets?

Yes! Academic teams can register datasets with standardized DOI identifiers, licensing tags, and reproducible code notebooks.

Is ResearchGrid free for academic institutions?

ResearchGrid is built as a non-profit open-access index dedicated to public scholarly discovery and transparent science.

How can researchers join the network?

Scholars can link their ORCID profile to auto-populate their publications, projects, and upcoming conference keynotes.
